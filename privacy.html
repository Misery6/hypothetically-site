Privacy Policy for Hypothetically

Last updated: September 2026

Hypothetically is made by Mohammad Al Rebh. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

We don't collect anything ourselves. The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics of our own, and we never see what you type into it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • a device identifier, such as Apple's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) or an identifier that is limited to this app or developer
  • advertising data, such as which ads you saw or tapped
  • how you interact with the app, such as when it is opened
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs and launch time

Google uses this for advertising and analytics. It is handled under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from partner apps is explained here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • Tracking. The app asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you say no, you still see ads, just less personalized ones.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and some other regions). Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice in the app under Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Pro. The one-time Pro purchase removes ads entirely. With Pro, no ads are requested at all.

The first two days of using the app never show ads.

Data on your device

The app saves your settings on your device only, such as your chosen theme, whether you've seen the introduction, and simple counters used to decide when an ad or a rating request may appear. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store whether you own Pro so it can unlock it.

Ratings

The app may occasionally show Apple's standard rating prompt. Apple handles it; we don't see who rated or what they wrote unless it's posted publicly on the App Store.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
